## Data Visualization Strategy

### Filter Chart:
- **Purpose**: Show filtered subset
- **Colors**: Purple/Orange theme
- **Aggregation**: Smart (daily/weekly/monthly based on range)
- **Comparison**: Current vs Last Year

### Main Chart:
- **Purpose**: Show overall trends
- **Colors**: Green/Blue theme
- **Aggregation**: User-selected period
- **Comparison**: Current vs Last Year
